Questions

Before you call.

Do you handle emergency plumbing?

Yes. The emergency line is intended for urgent leaks, burst pipes, blocked drains and water-system failures.

Do you work with commercial properties?

Yes. The site is designed to support residential customers as well as offices, facilities, developments and property teams.

Can I get a quote before planned work?

Yes. Planned work can begin with a clear scope and recommended next step before installation or repair proceeds.

What should I do during a major leak?

If safe, shut off the nearest water supply, protect electrical areas from water and call the emergency line. Avoid attempting a complex repair yourself.

Need help with a plumbing problem?
