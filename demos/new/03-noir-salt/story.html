Our story

Built around
the fire.

We opened Noir & Salt with one belief: a restaurant should feel alive. Our cooks work around the open fire, our menu follows the seasons, and our room is designed for long dinners rather than quick transactions.

We source from nearby farms, small fisheries and producers we know by name. The result changes often—and that is exactly the point.
