WHY DOES YOUR WEBSITE NEED SEO?

A website alone doesn't guarantee customers will find you.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) helps search engines understand your website and helps your business appear when potential customers search for products or services that you offer. For example, a plumber in New York may want to appear for “Plumber near me”, “Emergency plumber in New York”, “Drain cleaning service” or “Water heater repair”. A properly optimized website can help your business compete for these searches and attract relevant visitors.
